SNEK Taiwan Website Featured on SNEK Timeline!

October 10, 2026 · 2 min read
SNEK Taiwan Community
Taiwan Community Contributors

A momentous milestone for the SNEK Taiwan community! We are thrilled to share that the official SNEK Taiwan website (snek.com.tw) was officially recognized and immortalized as a global Community Milestone on SNEK Timeline on October 9, 2026!

About SNEK Timeline​

SNEK Timeline is the authoritative historical chronicle maintained by The Snekverse (@snekcoinprice) and dedicated contributors. It chronicles every significant event in SNEK history since its fair launch in 2023 — including major ecosystem launches, Tier-1 exchange listings, global merchandise partnerships, and regional community milestones.

Being featured in this official chronicle signifies that our local Taiwan grassroots movement is fully acknowledged and celebrated across the international Cardano and SNEK ecosystems.

Feature Summary on SNEK Timeline​

Under the October 9, 2026 timeline entry, the milestone is documented as "SNEK Taiwan Website Officially Live!":

"Snek 1u announced the official launch of the SNEK Taiwan community website at snek.com.tw. The Traditional Chinese hub targets local Web3 enthusiasts with token resources, ecosystem updates, and community guidelines focused on positive discussion. It also directs users to a LINE group to grow the regional Snek presence on Cardano."

—— Excerpt from SNEK Timeline

The chronicle highlights the initiative led by community admin SNEK 1u, acknowledging our mission to provide accurate education, lower Web3 barriers, and unite local holders through our thriving LINE hub.

Gratitude to the Entire Taiwan Snek Army​

This achievement belongs to every supporter and community builder:

  • Sincere gratitude to admin SNEK 1u (Threads: @snek_1u) for leadership and coordination.
  • Thank you to all volunteers contributing to translations, copywriting, web design, and meme creations.
  • A huge shout-out to all community members keeping discussions friendly, rational, and full of positive vibes!

Looking Forward​

Being included on SNEK Timeline is a huge honor, but our journey is just getting started! We will continue refining educational guides, tracking Cardano on-chain innovations, and expanding fun community events.
