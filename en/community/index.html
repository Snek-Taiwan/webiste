Community Vision & Mission

SNEK is more than just a token; it represents grassroots community strength and Web3 cultural bonding on Cardano. The Taiwan community's mission is to spread the joyful, friendly, and open SNEK meme culture, lower entry barriers for newcomers, and build the warmest bridge between global crypto space and local Taiwanese friends.

Community Guidelines

1. No FUD Allowed (Strong Consensus)

Refrain from spreading unverified panic or emotional fudding. Ride market volatility with rational humor and mutual support to protect community morale.

2. SNEK & Casual Banter Only (Stay Pure)

Focus on SNEK ecosystem milestones, on-chain culture, and casual daily life chats. Unrelated project shilling, DM scams, and spam are strictly forbidden.

3. Entry Verification & Friendly Vibes (State 1u Position)

Please state your SNEK 1u holding position upon applying. Warmly embrace beginner questions, communicate rationally, and always Do Your Own Research (DYOR).
